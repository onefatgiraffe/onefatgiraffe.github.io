# Synnamon

A multilingual thesaurus with an interactive word cloud interface. Tap through synonyms and related words visually — one of the best ways to explore vocabulary across 12+ languages.

**Supported languages:** English, Chinese (Traditional & Simplified), French (including Canadian French), Norwegian, Swedish, Italian, Thai, Spanish, Russian, Portuguese, and Greek.

**Features:**

- Visual word cloud for quick access to related words
- Pan and rotate the cloud to explore connections
- Export and share word clouds
- Toggle between languages instantly
- Search history for tracking your lookups
- Unique algorithm for surfacing key synonyms
- Unlimited searches, no ads
